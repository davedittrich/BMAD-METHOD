# Debate Structure Patterns

## Oxford-Style Debate

- **Opening Statements** (6-8 minutes each side)
- **First Rebuttals** (4-5 minutes each side)
- **Second Rebuttals** (3-4 minutes each side)
- **Closing Arguments** (2-3 minutes each side)

## Parliamentary Debate (British Parliamentary)

- **Opening Government** (7 minutes): Defines motion, presents case
- **Opening Opposition** (8 minutes): Refutes, presents counter-case
- **Member Government** (8 minutes): Extends government case
- **Member Opposition** (8 minutes): Extends opposition case
- **Opposition Whip** (4 minutes): Summation for opposition
- **Government Whip** (5 minutes): Final summation for government

## Lincoln-Douglas Format

- **Affirmative Constructive** (6 minutes): Value framework and case
- **Cross-Examination** (3 minutes): Negative questions affirmative
- **Negative Constructive** (7 minutes): Counter-framework and refutation
- **Cross-Examination** (3 minutes): Affirmative questions negative
- **First Affirmative Rebuttal** (4 minutes): Responds and rebuilds
- **Negative Rebuttal** (6 minutes): Crystallizes key voting issues
- **Second Affirmative Rebuttal** (3 minutes): Final summation

## Public Forum Debate

- **Team A Constructive** (4 minutes): Opening case
- **Team B Constructive** (4 minutes): Counter-case
- **Crossfire** (3 minutes): Questions between teams
- **Team A Rebuttal** (4 minutes): Defends and attacks
- **Team B Rebuttal** (4 minutes): Defends and attacks
- **Crossfire** (3 minutes): Second round questions
- **Team A Summary** (3 minutes): Key arguments
- **Team B Summary** (3 minutes): Key arguments
- **Grand Crossfire** (3 minutes): Open discussion
- **Team A Final Focus** (2 minutes): Voting issues
- **Team B Final Focus** (2 minutes): Final voting issues

## Informal Conversation Debate

- **Introductions** (2-5 minutes): Topic introduction and participant backgrounds
- **Opening Positions** (5-10 minutes each): Initial stance presentation
- **Interactive Discussion** (30-60 minutes): Free-flowing argument exchange
- **Moderated Segments** (5-15 minutes): Focused topic exploration
- **Closing Statements** (3-5 minutes each): Final position summary

## Podcast-Style Debate

- **Setup** (5-10 minutes): Context, participants, ground rules
- **Position Statements** (10-15 minutes each): Detailed opening arguments
- **Cross-Discussion** (45-90 minutes): Back-and-forth examination
- **Audience Questions** (15-30 minutes): Q&A if applicable  
- **Wrap-up** (5-10 minutes): Final thoughts and next steps

## Town Hall Format

- **Moderator Introduction** (2-3 minutes): Rules and format explanation
- **Opening Statements** (3-5 minutes each): Position presentation
- **Audience Questions** (30-45 minutes): Moderated Q&A
- **Direct Exchange** (15-20 minutes): Participant interaction
- **Closing Statements** (2-3 minutes each): Final appeals

## Academic Conference Style

- **Paper Presentation** (15-20 minutes): Detailed argument with evidence
- **Respondent Commentary** (10-15 minutes): Academic critique
- **Author Response** (5-10 minutes): Defense and clarification
- **Audience Q&A** (15-25 minutes): Scholarly discussion
- **Moderator Summary** (3-5 minutes): Key points synthesis
