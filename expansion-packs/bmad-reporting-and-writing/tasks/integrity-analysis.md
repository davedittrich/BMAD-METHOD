# integrity-analysis

## Task Overview

Apply Stephen L. Carter's integrity framework to systematically analyze the integrity of individuals, organizations, or actions through discernment, acknowledgment, and action consistency.

## Instructions

1. **Step 1: Discernment Assessment**
   - Evaluate moral awareness and ethical reasoning
   - Look for evidence of distinguishing right from wrong
   - Assess understanding of ethical implications
   - Document examples of moral discernment or lack thereof

2. **Step 2: Acknowledgment Assessment**
   - Examine consistency between stated values and actions
   - Look for evidence of acting on moral convictions
   - Assess willingness to bear costs for doing right
   - Identify any patterns of moral compromise

3. **Step 3: Action Consistency Assessment**
   - Evaluate public vs. private behavior alignment
   - Assess transparency in moral reasoning
   - Look for accountability and responsibility-taking
   - Check ability to defend actions on moral grounds

4. **Pattern Analysis**
   - Identify consistent behavioral patterns
   - Note any changes over time
   - Assess responses to moral challenges
   - Evaluate learning from moral failures

5. **Contextual Factors**
   - Consider external pressures and constraints
   - Assess available information and options
   - Note cultural and historical context
   - Evaluate competing moral obligations

6. **Documentation and Evidence**
   - Compile specific examples and evidence
   - Cross-reference multiple sources
   - Verify accuracy of claims and incidents
   - Maintain objectivity in assessment
