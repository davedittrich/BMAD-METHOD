# identify-fallacies

## Task Overview

Systematically identify logical fallacies in arguments, reasoning, or written content to assess logical validity and strength.

## Instructions

1. **Fallacy Recognition**
   - Scan for common formal fallacies (invalid logical structure)
   - Identify informal fallacies (misleading or irrelevant arguments)
   - Note statistical and probabilistic fallacies
   - Look for rhetorical fallacies and manipulation techniques

2. **Documentation Process**
   - Quote exact fallacious statements
   - Identify the specific type of fallacy
   - Explain why it constitutes a logical error
   - Assess impact on overall argument strength

3. **Context Analysis**
   - Consider whether apparent fallacies are intentional or accidental
   - Assess if fallacies undermine key arguments or minor points
   - Note patterns suggesting systematic logical weakness
   - Evaluate overall logical reasoning quality

## Output Requirements

Produce fallacy identification report with specific examples and explanations.
