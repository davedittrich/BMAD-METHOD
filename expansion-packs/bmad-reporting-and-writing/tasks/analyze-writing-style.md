# analyze-writing-style

## Task Overview

Systematically analyze writing samples to identify style patterns, voice characteristics, and linguistic preferences for consistency and replication.

## Instructions

1. **Style Pattern Analysis**
   - Identify sentence structure preferences (simple, complex, compound)
   - Analyze paragraph organization and length patterns
   - Note transition patterns and connecting phrases
   - Examine overall flow and pacing preferences

2. **Voice Characteristics**
   - Determine tone (formal, casual, conversational, authoritative)
   - Identify personality traits reflected in writing
   - Note emotional register and expression patterns
   - Assess level of directness vs. diplomatic language

3. **Vocabulary Analysis**
   - Identify commonly used words and phrases
   - Note specialized terminology and jargon usage
   - Analyze complexity level and reading grade
   - Document unique expressions or verbal habits

4. **Grammar and Mechanics**
   - Note punctuation preferences and patterns
   - Identify grammar quirks or consistent choices
   - Analyze use of active vs. passive voice
   - Document capitalization and formatting preferences

5. **Rhetorical Devices**
   - Identify use of metaphors, analogies, and examples
   - Note question usage and rhetorical techniques
   - Analyze humor, irony, and wit patterns
   - Document storytelling and narrative elements

6. **Audience Adaptation**
   - Assess how style changes for different contexts
   - Note formality level adjustments
   - Identify consistent elements across contexts
   - Document platform-specific adaptations
