# analyze-logical-structure

## Task Overview

Systematically analyze the logical structure of arguments, identifying premises, conclusions, logical flow, and potential weaknesses in reasoning.

## Instructions

1. **Argument Identification**
   - Identify the main conclusion or thesis
   - Extract all supporting premises and sub-arguments
   - Distinguish between explicit and implicit premises
   - Map the overall argument structure

2. **Logical Analysis**
   - Assess the validity of logical connections
   - Check if conclusions follow from premises
   - Identify any logical gaps or jumps
   - Evaluate the strength of inductive reasoning

3. **Evidence Evaluation**
   - Assess the quality and relevance of evidence
   - Check for sufficient support for each claim
   - Identify unsupported assertions
   - Evaluate the credibility of sources cited

4. **Fallacy Detection**
   - Identify common logical fallacies
   - Note ad hominem attacks or straw man arguments
   - Check for false dilemmas or slippery slope reasoning
   - Flag circular reasoning or appeals to emotion

5. **Counter-Argument Analysis**
   - Consider alternative interpretations of evidence
   - Identify potential objections to the argument
   - Assess how well counter-arguments are addressed
   - Note any cherry-picking or selective reasoning

6. **Overall Assessment**
   - Rate the logical strength of the argument
   - Identify the strongest and weakest points
   - Suggest improvements to logical structure
   - Recommend additional evidence needed
