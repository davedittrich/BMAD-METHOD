# historical-research

## Task Overview

Conduct systematic historical research to provide context, background, and timeline information for current topics or subjects.

## Instructions

1. **Research Scope Definition**
   - Define the historical period relevant to the topic
   - Identify key events, figures, and developments
   - Establish geographical and cultural boundaries
   - Determine level of detail needed

2. **Timeline Development**
   - Create chronological sequence of relevant events
   - Identify cause-and-effect relationships
   - Note concurrent developments and influences
   - Establish historical precedents and patterns

3. **Primary Source Research**
   - Locate contemporary documents and records
   - Find firsthand accounts and testimonies
   - Access historical archives and databases
   - Verify authenticity and provenance of sources

4. **Secondary Source Analysis**
   - Review scholarly interpretations and analyses
   - Compare different historical perspectives
   - Identify areas of scholarly consensus and debate
   - Note evolution of historical understanding

5. **Context Analysis**
   - Examine social, political, and cultural context
   - Identify relevant economic and technological factors
   - Assess influence of key institutions and movements
   - Consider international and regional factors

6. **Relevance Assessment**
   - Connect historical patterns to current situation
   - Identify lessons learned and precedents
   - Note similarities and differences with past
   - Assess predictive value of historical patterns

## Output Requirements

Deliver comprehensive historical research including:

- Detailed timeline of relevant events
- Analysis of historical context and patterns
- Connection to current topic or situation
- Bibliography of sources consulted
