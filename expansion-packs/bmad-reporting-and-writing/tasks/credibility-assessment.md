# credibility-assessment

## Task Overview

Systematically evaluate the credibility and trustworthiness of individuals, organizations, or institutions using established assessment criteria.

## Instructions

1. **Expertise Evaluation**
   - Assess relevant qualifications and education
   - Review professional experience and track record
   - Examine peer recognition and awards
   - Evaluate specialized knowledge and expertise

2. **Track Record Analysis**
   - Review history of accuracy and reliability
   - Examine past mistakes and how they were handled
   - Assess consistency of performance over time
   - Note any patterns of success or failure

3. **Independence Assessment**
   - Identify potential conflicts of interest
   - Assess financial incentives and pressures
   - Evaluate institutional loyalties and obligations
   - Check for bias sources and motivations

4. **Transparency Evaluation**
   - Assess openness about methods and sources
   - Review disclosure of conflicts and limitations
   - Examine willingness to admit uncertainty
   - Note transparency in correction and retraction policies

5. **Verification and Accountability**
   - Check if claims can be independently verified
   - Assess accountability mechanisms in place
   - Review response to challenges and criticism
   - Examine correction and update practices

6. **Contextual Factors**
   - Consider relevant situational pressures
   - Assess cultural and institutional context
   - Note any extraordinary circumstances
   - Evaluate available resources and constraints

- How will the results be used?

**Available Information:**

- What information is available about the subject?
- Are there specific sources or databases to consult?
- What level of investigation is possible or appropriate?

## Output Requirements

Deliver comprehensive credibility assessment including:

- Detailed evaluation against each assessment criterion
- Overall credibility rating with clear justification
- Specific strengths and weaknesses identified
- Recommendations for reliance or verification needs
