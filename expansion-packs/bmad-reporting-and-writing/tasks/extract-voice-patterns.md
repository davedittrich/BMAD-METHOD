# extract-voice-patterns

## Task Overview

Analyze writing samples to extract distinctive voice patterns, style characteristics, and linguistic fingerprints for consistency analysis and authorship verification.

## Instructions

1. **Linguistic Pattern Analysis**
   - Identify sentence structure preferences and patterns
   - Analyze vocabulary choices and complexity levels
   - Examine punctuation and formatting habits
   - Note dialect, colloquialisms, or regional variations

2. **Style Characteristics**
   - Assess tone and emotional register patterns
   - Identify formal vs. informal writing tendencies
   - Examine use of humor, sarcasm, or irony
   - Note persuasive techniques and rhetorical devices

3. **Structural Elements**
   - Analyze paragraph organization patterns
   - Examine transition and flow preferences
   - Identify introduction and conclusion styles
   - Note use of headers, lists, and formatting

4. **Content Patterns**
   - Identify recurring themes and topics
   - Examine perspective and viewpoint consistency
   - Note citation and reference habits
   - Assess use of examples and illustrations

5. **Technical Elements**
   - Analyze grammar and syntax patterns
   - Examine error patterns and corrections
   - Note capitalization and spacing habits
   - Identify typing or composition quirks

6. **Voice Evolution**
   - Track changes in voice over time periods
   - Note adaptations for different audiences
   - Identify consistent core characteristics
   - Assess authenticity versus adaptation

## Output Requirements

Deliver comprehensive voice pattern analysis including:

- Detailed linguistic fingerprint profile
- Style characteristic summary with examples
- Pattern consistency assessment across samples
- Distinctive markers and unique identifiers
- Voice evolution timeline if applicable
