# optimize-retention

## Task Overview

Optimize video content for audience retention using proven techniques for engagement, pacing, and psychological triggers.

## Instructions

1. **Hook Optimization**
   - Strengthen opening 15 seconds with compelling promise
   - Use pattern interrupts and surprising statements
   - Create immediate value demonstration
   - Establish clear expectations and payoff

2. **Retention Curve Analysis**
   - Identify high-dropout risk sections
   - Plan engagement spikes every 30-60 seconds
   - Create curiosity gaps and open loops
   - Use preview techniques to maintain interest

3. **Pacing and Energy**
   - Vary pacing to maintain attention
   - Increase energy at retention weak points
   - Use strategic pauses and emphasis
   - Plan visual and audio variety

4. **Psychological Engagement**
   - Use social proof and relatability
   - Create emotional investment in outcomes
   - Apply curiosity and completion psychology
   - Include interactive mental exercises

5. **Content Structure**
   - Break content into digestible segments
   - Use clear signposting and transitions
   - Create micro-rewards throughout content
   - Build toward satisfying conclusion

6. **Platform-Specific Optimization**
   - Apply platform retention best practices
   - Consider algorithm preferences and signals
   - Optimize for average view duration
   - Plan for platform-specific engagement features
