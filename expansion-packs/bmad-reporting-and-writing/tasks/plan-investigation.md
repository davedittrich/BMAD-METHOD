# plan-investigation

## Task Overview

Develop a comprehensive research strategy and investigation plan for complex topics, ensuring systematic coverage and reliable methodology.

## Instructions

1. **Research Question Development**
   - Define the central research question or hypothesis
   - Break down into specific sub-questions
   - Identify key information gaps to address
   - Establish scope and boundaries of investigation

2. **Source Strategy Planning**
   - Identify types of sources needed (primary, secondary, expert)
   - Plan approach for accessing key sources
   - Develop strategy for source verification
   - Create backup plans for difficult-to-access information

3. **Timeline and Methodology**
   - Establish realistic timeline with milestones
   - Plan research phases and sequence
   - Define methodology for data collection
   - Build in time for verification and cross-checking

4. **Resource Assessment**
   - Identify required tools and resources
   - Plan for potential costs or access barriers
   - Consider expertise or consultation needs
   - Assess legal and ethical constraints

5. **Quality Control Framework**
   - Establish verification standards
   - Plan cross-referencing methodology
   - Define criteria for source credibility
   - Create documentation standards

6. **Risk Assessment**
   - Identify potential investigative risks
   - Plan for incomplete or contradictory information
   - Consider legal or safety concerns
   - Develop contingency plans

- Research strategy and methodology
- Timeline with specific milestones
- Source identification and access strategy
- Quality control and verification framework
