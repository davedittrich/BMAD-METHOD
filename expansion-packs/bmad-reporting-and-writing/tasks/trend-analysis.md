# trend-analysis

## Task Overview

Analyze patterns, trends, and developments over time to provide context and predictive insights for current topics.

## Instructions

1. **Data Collection**
   - Gather historical data relevant to the topic
   - Identify key metrics and indicators
   - Establish timeline parameters
   - Collect from multiple reliable sources

2. **Pattern Recognition**
   - Identify recurring patterns and cycles
   - Note acceleration or deceleration trends
   - Recognize seasonal or periodic variations
   - Spot anomalies and outliers

3. **Trend Analysis**
   - Calculate trend directions and magnitudes
   - Identify trend reversals and inflection points
   - Assess trend strength and reliability
   - Project potential future developments

4. **Contextual Factors**
   - Identify external influences on trends
   - Consider multiple causative factors
   - Assess predictive reliability
   - Note limitations and uncertainties

## Output Requirements

Create comprehensive trend analysis with data visualization recommendations and predictive insights.
