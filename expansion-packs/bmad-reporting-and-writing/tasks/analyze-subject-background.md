# analyze-subject-background

## Task Overview

Conduct comprehensive background analysis of individuals or organizations to understand their history, motivations, credibility patterns, and integrity indicators.

## Instructions

1. **Personal/Organizational History**
   - Research educational background and credentials
   - Examine career progression and key positions
   - Identify major achievements and failures
   - Map timeline of significant events and decisions

2. **Professional Track Record**
   - Assess expertise claims and actual performance
   - Review past statements and their accuracy
   - Examine consistency between stated values and actions
   - Note any changes in positions or affiliations

3. **Financial and Business Interests**
   - Identify funding sources and revenue streams
   - Map business relationships and partnerships
   - Assess potential financial motivations
   - Check for conflicts of interest

4. **Public Statements and Positions**
   - Catalog major public statements and positions
   - Track evolution of stated beliefs over time
   - Note contradictions or inconsistencies
   - Examine response to criticism or challenges

5. **Network and Associations**
   - Map key relationships and associations
   - Identify mentors, peers, and collaborators
   - Note organizational affiliations and roles
   - Assess influence networks and power structures

6. **Reputation and Recognition**
   - Review peer recognition and awards
   - Assess media coverage and public perception
   - Note any controversies or scandals
   - Check professional standing and credentials

7. **Integrity Assessment**
   - Evaluate consistency between words and actions
   - Assess honesty in admitting mistakes or limitations
   - Check transparency in disclosure of conflicts
   - Review ethical conduct and decision-making patterns
   - Examine response to ethical dilemmas
   - Note any patterns of deception or misrepresentation

## Output Requirements

Deliver comprehensive background analysis including:

- Chronological overview of key events and decisions
- Assessment of credibility and reliability patterns
- Integrity evaluation with specific examples
- Identification of potential motivations and biases
- Summary of strengths, weaknesses, and areas of concern
