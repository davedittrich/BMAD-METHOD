# track-record-analysis

## Task Overview

Systematically analyze the historical performance, accuracy, reliability patterns, and integrity evolution of individuals or organizations to assess their track record.

## Instructions

1. **Performance History**
   - Document key decisions and their outcomes
   - Track success rates across different domains
   - Identify patterns of performance over time
   - Note any significant improvements or deteriorations

2. **Accuracy Assessment**
   - Review past predictions and their accuracy
   - Examine fact-checking records and corrections
   - Assess reliability of reported information
   - Track evolution of positions on key issues

3. **Consistency Analysis**
   - Compare stated principles with actual actions
   - Identify contradictions or changes in positions
   - Assess reliability of commitments and promises
   - Note patterns of behavior under pressure

4. **Integrity Assessment Over Time**
   - Track ethical decision-making patterns across career/history
   - Assess consistency of moral positions under different circumstances
   - Examine transparency and honesty evolution over time
   - Note any integrity improvements or deteriorations
   - Review responses to ethical dilemmas at different periods
   - Assess trustworthiness patterns with various stakeholders

5. **Error Handling**
   - Review how mistakes and errors were handled
   - Assess willingness to admit and correct errors
   - Examine learning from past mistakes
   - Note transparency in acknowledging limitations

6. **Stakeholder Impact**
   - Assess impact on various stakeholder groups
   - Review feedback from clients, partners, or peers
   - Examine long-term relationships and trust levels
   - Note any patterns of satisfied or dissatisfied stakeholders

7. **Context Consideration**
   - Account for external factors affecting performance
   - Assess performance relative to peers or benchmarks
   - Consider resource constraints and circumstances
   - Note any exceptional conditions or challenges

**Analysis Parameters:**

- What specific metrics or outcomes should be evaluated?
- Are there particular decisions or actions to focus on?
- What comparison benchmarks should be used?

**Evidence Sources:**

- What sources are available for performance data?
- Are there specific records or documentation to review?
- What verification methods should be applied?

## Output Requirements

Deliver comprehensive track record analysis including:

- Performance summary with key statistics and trends
- Accuracy assessment with specific examples
- Consistency evaluation and pattern identification
- Integrity assessment showing evolution over time
- Error handling and learning capability assessment
- Overall reliability rating with supporting evidence
