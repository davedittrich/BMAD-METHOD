# Research Methodology Checklist

Use this checklist to ensure rigorous and systematic research methodology in investigations and analysis.

## Research Planning and Design

### Research Question Definition

- [ ] **Clear research question**: Primary research question is clearly defined and focused
- [ ] **Specific objectives**: Specific research objectives are identified and measurable
- [ ] **Scope definition**: Research scope and boundaries are clearly established
- [ ] **Success criteria**: Clear criteria for successful completion are defined
- [ ] **Feasibility assessment**: Research is feasible given available time and resources

### Literature Review Planning

- [ ] **Comprehensive scope**: Literature review covers all relevant areas and perspectives
- [ ] **Source diversity**: Multiple types of sources included (academic, professional, governmental)
- [ ] **Time frame coverage**: Appropriate historical time frame covered
- [ ] **Geographic coverage**: Relevant geographic regions included in review
- [ ] **Language considerations**: Non-English sources considered when relevant

### Research Design Framework

- [ ] **Methodology selection**: Appropriate research methodology chosen for the question
- [ ] **Mixed methods consideration**: Combination of qualitative and quantitative methods considered
- [ ] **Bias prevention**: Design includes measures to prevent and identify bias
- [ ] **Ethical considerations**: Ethical implications identified and addressed
- [ ] **Validation methods**: Methods for validating findings are planned

## Source Identification and Evaluation

### Source Discovery Strategy

- [ ] **Database coverage**: Relevant academic and professional databases searched
- [ ] **Search term optimization**: Comprehensive and strategic search terms used
- [ ] **Citation tracking**: Citation chains followed to discover additional sources
- [ ] **Expert consultation**: Subject matter experts consulted for source recommendations
- [ ] **Gray literature**: Government reports, white papers, and other gray literature included

### Source Quality Assessment

- [ ] **Credibility evaluation**: Source credibility systematically assessed
- [ ] **Peer review status**: Peer-reviewed sources prioritized when available
- [ ] **Author expertise**: Author qualifications and expertise evaluated
- [ ] **Publication standards**: Publication venue standards and reputation considered
- [ ] **Recency assessment**: Currency and relevance of sources evaluated

### Source Documentation

- [ ] **Complete citations**: All sources include complete bibliographic information
- [ ] **Access information**: Information about how to access sources provided
- [ ] **Retrieval dates**: Dates of access recorded for online sources
- [ ] **Version control**: Version numbers or edition information recorded
- [ ] **Archive locations**: Permanent archive locations noted when available

## Data Collection Methodology

### Primary Data Collection

- [ ] **Interview protocols**: Structured interview protocols developed and tested
- [ ] **Survey design**: Surveys designed with appropriate validation and testing
- [ ] **Observation methods**: Systematic observation protocols established
- [ ] **Document analysis**: Systematic methods for analyzing primary documents
- [ ] **Data recording**: Consistent methods for recording and storing primary data

### Secondary Data Collection

- [ ] **Data source verification**: Secondary data sources verified for accuracy
- [ ] **Data completeness**: Completeness of secondary data assessed
- [ ] **Data consistency**: Consistency across different secondary sources checked
- [ ] **Update frequency**: How frequently secondary data is updated determined
- [ ] **Data limitations**: Limitations of secondary data sources identified

### Sampling Methodology

- [ ] **Population definition**: Target population clearly defined
- [ ] **Sampling method**: Appropriate sampling method selected and justified
- [ ] **Sample size**: Adequate sample size determined using statistical principles
- [ ] **Representativeness**: Sample representativeness assessed and documented
- [ ] **Bias mitigation**: Steps taken to minimize sampling bias

## Information Verification and Validation

### Fact-Checking Procedures

- [ ] **Multiple source verification**: Key facts verified through multiple independent sources
- [ ] **Primary source tracing**: Claims traced back to original primary sources
- [ ] **Expert verification**: Critical information verified by subject matter experts
- [ ] **Statistical verification**: Statistical claims and calculations independently verified
- [ ] **Timeline verification**: Chronological accuracy of events and claims verified

### Cross-Referencing Methods

- [ ] **Source triangulation**: Information triangulated across different types of sources
- [ ] **Method triangulation**: Multiple research methods used to verify findings
- [ ] **Investigator triangulation**: Multiple investigators involved in verification process
- [ ] **Theory triangulation**: Findings examined from multiple theoretical perspectives
- [ ] **Data triangulation**: Different types of data used to support conclusions

### Quality Control Measures

- [ ] **Systematic documentation**: All verification steps systematically documented
- [ ] **Chain of evidence**: Clear chain of evidence maintained for all claims
- [ ] **Peer review**: Research methods and findings subjected to peer review
- [ ] **External audit**: Independent external review of methodology and findings
- [ ] **Replication potential**: Methods documented sufficiently for replication

## Bias Recognition and Mitigation

### Researcher Bias Prevention

- [ ] **Self-awareness assessment**: Researcher biases and assumptions identified
- [ ] **Bracketing techniques**: Techniques used to bracket personal biases
- [ ] **Multiple investigators**: Multiple investigators used to reduce individual bias
- [ ] **Devil's advocate**: Formal devil's advocate process implemented
- [ ] **Blind review**: Blind or double-blind review processes used where possible

### Selection Bias Prevention

- [ ] **Systematic selection**: Sources and data selected using systematic criteria
- [ ] **Inclusion criteria**: Clear inclusion and exclusion criteria established
- [ ] **Search strategy documentation**: Comprehensive documentation of search strategies
- [ ] **Negative case analysis**: Active search for disconfirming evidence
- [ ] **Outlier consideration**: Outlier cases and contradictory evidence included

### Confirmation Bias Mitigation

- [ ] **Hypothesis testing**: Formal hypothesis testing rather than confirmation seeking
- [ ] **Alternative explanations**: Alternative explanations actively considered
- [ ] **Contradictory evidence**: Systematic search for contradictory evidence
- [ ] **Competing theories**: Multiple competing theories evaluated
- [ ] **Falsification attempts**: Attempts made to falsify rather than confirm hypotheses

## Data Analysis and Interpretation

### Analytical Framework

- [ ] **Clear analytical framework**: Analytical framework clearly defined and justified
- [ ] **Coding systems**: Systematic coding systems developed for qualitative data
- [ ] **Statistical methods**: Appropriate statistical methods selected and applied
- [ ] **Pattern recognition**: Systematic methods for identifying patterns and themes
- [ ] **Interpretation guidelines**: Clear guidelines for interpretation established

### Validity and Reliability

- [ ] **Internal validity**: Measures taken to ensure internal validity
- [ ] **External validity**: Generalizability and external validity assessed
- [ ] **Construct validity**: Concepts and constructs properly defined and measured
- [ ] **Content validity**: Content validity of measures assessed
- [ ] **Reliability testing**: Reliability of data collection and analysis methods tested

### Uncertainty Management

- [ ] **Uncertainty identification**: Areas of uncertainty clearly identified
- [ ] **Confidence intervals**: Appropriate confidence intervals calculated and reported
- [ ] **Sensitivity analysis**: Sensitivity analysis conducted for key findings
- [ ] **Robustness testing**: Findings tested for robustness across different assumptions
- [ ] **Limitation acknowledgment**: Limitations clearly acknowledged and discussed

## Ethical Considerations

### Research Ethics

- [ ] **IRB approval**: Institutional Review Board approval obtained if required
- [ ] **Informed consent**: Informed consent obtained from research participants
- [ ] **Privacy protection**: Privacy and confidentiality of sources protected
- [ ] **Harm prevention**: Measures taken to prevent harm to research subjects
- [ ] **Voluntary participation**: Participation is voluntary and coercion-free

### Professional Standards

- [ ] **Professional codes**: Relevant professional codes of ethics followed
- [ ] **Plagiarism prevention**: Proper attribution given to all sources and ideas
- [ ] **Data integrity**: Data integrity maintained throughout research process
- [ ] **Transparency requirements**: Transparency requirements met for the field
- [ ] **Conflict disclosure**: Any conflicts of interest disclosed appropriately

### Social Responsibility

- [ ] **Public benefit**: Research serves legitimate public or scientific benefit
- [ ] **Cultural sensitivity**: Cultural norms and sensitivities respected
- [ ] **Power dynamics**: Power dynamics between researcher and subjects considered
- [ ] **Vulnerable populations**: Special protections for vulnerable populations implemented
- [ ] **Community impact**: Impact on research communities and subjects considered

## Documentation and Reporting

### Methodology Documentation

- [ ] **Complete methodology**: All methodological decisions documented and justified
- [ ] **Procedure replication**: Procedures documented sufficiently for replication
- [ ] **Decision trail**: Decision-making process throughout research documented
- [ ] **Changes tracking**: Any changes to methodology tracked and explained
- [ ] **Quality assurance**: Quality assurance procedures documented

### Results Presentation

- [ ] **Objective presentation**: Results presented objectively without editorializing
- [ ] **Appropriate visualizations**: Data visualized appropriately for the audience
- [ ] **Statistical significance**: Statistical significance properly reported and interpreted
- [ ] **Effect sizes**: Effect sizes reported along with statistical significance
- [ ] **Confidence intervals**: Confidence intervals reported for key estimates

### Transparency and Accessibility

- [ ] **Method transparency**: Methods clearly explained for non-specialist audiences
- [ ] **Data availability**: Raw data made available where ethically and legally possible
- [ ] **Reproducibility**: Research designed and reported for reproducibility
- [ ] **Open access**: Results made available through open access when possible
- [ ] **Plain language summary**: Plain language summary provided for public audiences

## Quality Assurance and Review

### Internal Quality Control

- [ ] **Systematic checking**: Systematic checking procedures implemented throughout
- [ ] **Double data entry**: Critical data entered and verified by multiple people
- [ ] **Calculation verification**: All calculations independently verified
- [ ] **Citation accuracy**: All citations checked for accuracy and completeness
- [ ] **Internal consistency**: Internal consistency of findings checked

### External Review

- [ ] **Peer review**: Research subjected to rigorous peer review
- [ ] **Expert consultation**: Subject matter experts consulted on methodology and findings
- [ ] **Stakeholder review**: Relevant stakeholders given opportunity to review findings
- [ ] **Public scrutiny**: Research made available for public scrutiny and comment
- [ ] **Replication encouragement**: Other researchers encouraged to replicate findings

### Continuous Improvement

- [ ] **Methodology refinement**: Methods continuously refined based on experience
- [ ] **Feedback incorporation**: Feedback from reviewers and users incorporated
- [ ] **Best practice adoption**: Current best practices adopted and implemented
- [ ] **Technology integration**: Appropriate new technologies integrated
- [ ] **Training updates**: Researcher training kept current with methodological advances

## Post-Research Activities

### Follow-up and Validation

- [ ] **Follow-up studies**: Follow-up studies planned to validate findings
- [ ] **Longitudinal tracking**: Longitudinal tracking implemented where appropriate
- [ ] **Impact assessment**: Impact of research findings tracked over time
- [ ] **Validity checking**: Long-term validity of findings periodically checked
- [ ] **Update procedures**: Procedures for updating findings as new information emerges

### Knowledge Dissemination

- [ ] **Multiple venues**: Results disseminated through multiple appropriate venues
- [ ] **Target audiences**: Different formats prepared for different target audiences
- [ ] **Public engagement**: Public engagement activities planned and implemented
- [ ] **Policy makers**: Relevant policy makers informed of findings
- [ ] **Practitioner community**: Practitioner community engaged with findings

### Legacy and Archival

- [ ] **Data preservation**: Research data preserved for future use
- [ ] **Method documentation**: Complete methodological documentation archived
- [ ] **Access procedures**: Clear procedures for future access to research materials
- [ ] **Ethics compliance**: Long-term ethics compliance procedures established
- [ ] **Institutional repository**: Research deposited in appropriate institutional repositories
