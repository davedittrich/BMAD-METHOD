# analyze-fallacies

## Task Overview

Systematically identify and analyze logical fallacies in written content using T. Edward Damer's "Attacking Faulty Reasoning" methodology, assessing arguments against the five criteria for good arguments.

## Instructions

1. **Apply Damer's Five Criteria Assessment**
   - **Structural**: Check for valid logical form and internal consistency
   - **Relevance**: Ensure premises provide genuine evidence for conclusion
   - **Acceptability**: Verify premises would be accepted by rational people
   - **Sufficiency**: Confirm adequate evidence quantity and quality
   - **Rebuttal**: Assess whether counterarguments are properly addressed

2. **Normal Form Reconstruction**
   - Identify and number all explicit premises clearly
   - Clearly state the main conclusion(s)
   - Map logical structure showing connections between premises and conclusions
   - Make hidden assumptions and unstated premises explicit
   - Check for gaps in reasoning and non-sequiturs

3. **Evidence Quality Evaluation**
   - Assess quality, relevance, and reliability of supporting evidence
   - Identify insufficient, misleading, or irrelevant evidence
   - Note cherry-picking, selective citation, or misrepresentation
   - Examine representativeness and validity of examples used
   - Check for proper source attribution and verification

4. **Fallacy Identification**
   - Identify specific fallacies by name and type
   - Document criterion violations for each fallacy
   - Assess severity and impact on overall argument
   - Note patterns of repeated fallacy types
   - Distinguish between logical errors and rhetorical choices

5. **Contextual Assessment**
   - Consider domain-specific reasoning norms
   - Assess audience and persuasive context
   - Note cultural factors affecting argument interpretation
   - Distinguish between formal logical errors and informal weaknesses

6. **Constructive Analysis (Code of Intellectual Conduct)**
   - Apply principle of charity to represent arguments in strongest form
   - Focus on strengthening arguments, not just criticizing
   - Provide specific suggestions for addressing identified problems
   - Assess whether arguments can be improved to meet all five criteria
   - Maintain intellectual humility and fairness in analysis

7. **Overall Assessment**
   - Rate logical strength using Damer's criteria (1-5 scale for each)
   - Identify strongest and weakest elements in the reasoning
   - Assess credibility and quality of evidence base
   - Note patterns in reasoning quality
   - Provide constructive recommendations for improvement

## Output Requirements

Deliver comprehensive logical fallacy analysis including:

- Argument structure diagram showing logical flow with numbered premises
- Assessment against each of Damer's five criteria with ratings
- Normal form reconstruction of main arguments
- Detailed inventory of fallacies with specific examples and quotations
- Assessment of logical validity, soundness, and evidence quality
- Application of Code of Intellectual Conduct principles
- Overall logical strength rating with detailed justification
- Identification of strongest and weakest reasoning elements
- Constructive recommendations for strengthening arguments to meet all criteria
