# guide-integrity-writing

## Task Overview

Provide real-time guidance during content drafting to ensure integrity consistency using Stephen L. Carter's framework, helping writers maintain intellectual honesty and moral clarity throughout the writing process.

## Instructions

1. **Discernment Guidance**
   - Help writer identify and articulate moral dimensions of the topic
   - Guide recognition of competing values and ethical considerations
   - Suggest questions to clarify what is right/wrong in the context
   - Prompt reflection on consequences and implications
   - Ensure moral awareness is present before taking positions

2. **Acting/Acknowledgment Guidance**
   - Help ensure consistency between stated values and advocated positions
   - Prompt writer to acknowledge costs and trade-offs of recommendations
   - Guide honest assessment of difficult choices
   - Suggest language that demonstrates principle over convenience
   - Help identify and avoid hypocrisy between claims and advocacy

3. **Transparency Guidance**
   - Encourage explicit statements of moral reasoning
   - Guide clear articulation of why positions are ethically sound
   - Suggest taking responsibility for moral stances
   - Help writer defend positions on moral (not just practical) grounds
   - Prompt disclosure of assumptions and value judgments

4. **Corruption Prevention**
   - Alert to moral relativism or convenient ethics
   - Flag potential double standards (in-group vs. out-group)
   - Warn against obscuring ethical reasoning
   - Identify blame-shifting language
   - Catch treating ethics as PR rather than principle

5. **Language and Framing Suggestions**
   - Suggest specific phrasings that demonstrate integrity
   - Provide examples of transparent moral reasoning
   - Recommend ways to acknowledge complexity honestly
   - Guide appropriate use of qualifiers and admissions of uncertainty
   - Help balance conviction with intellectual humility

6. **Structural Guidance**
   - Suggest where to address ethical dimensions in the piece
   - Guide placement of acknowledgments and disclosures
   - Recommend organization that supports transparency
   - Help ensure moral reasoning is woven throughout, not isolated

## Elicitation Requirements

elicit: true
format: |
Please provide:

**Content Context:**

- What are you writing about? (topic, argument, position)
- What is the purpose of this piece? (persuade, inform, analyze)
- Who is the intended audience?

**Current Draft Stage:**

- Are you outlining, drafting, or revising?
- Do you have existing text to review, or starting fresh?
- What sections or arguments need integrity guidance?

**Specific Concerns:**

- Are there particular integrity issues you're worried about?
- Are you taking controversial positions that need careful handling?
- Do you have competing values or trade-offs to navigate?

## Output Requirements

Provide real-time writing guidance including:

- Specific suggestions for maintaining integrity throughout the piece
- Recommended phrasings and language for transparent moral reasoning
- Alerts to potential integrity problems with suggested corrections
- Questions to help writer clarify and strengthen ethical positions
- Structural recommendations for integrating integrity considerations
- Examples of strong integrity-based argumentation relevant to the topic
- Checkpoint reminders for each of Carter's three steps
