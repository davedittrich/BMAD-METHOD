# analyze-subject-background

## Task Overview

Conduct comprehensive background analysis of individuals or organizations to understand their history, motivations, credibility patterns, and integrity indicators for informed content creation and source evaluation.

## Instructions

1. **Personal/Organizational History**
   - Research educational background and credentials
   - Examine career progression and key positions held
   - Identify major achievements and notable failures
   - Map timeline of significant events and pivotal decisions
   - Track evolution of roles, responsibilities, and influence

2. **Professional Track Record**
   - Assess expertise claims against actual demonstrated performance
   - Review past statements and predictions for accuracy
   - Examine consistency between stated values and actions taken
   - Note any significant changes in positions or affiliations over time
   - Document areas of genuine expertise vs. areas of overstated competence

3. **Financial and Business Interests**
   - Identify primary funding sources and revenue streams
   - Map business relationships, partnerships, and dependencies
   - Assess potential financial motivations and incentives
   - Check for conflicts of interest that may affect credibility
   - Examine transparency in financial disclosures

4. **Public Statements and Positions**
   - Catalog major public statements and declared positions
   - Track evolution of stated beliefs and positions over time
   - Note contradictions, inconsistencies, or unexplained reversals
   - Examine responses to criticism, challenges, or contrary evidence
   - Assess willingness to admit errors or update positions

5. **Network and Associations**
   - Map key relationships and professional associations
   - Identify mentors, peers, collaborators, and adversaries
   - Note organizational affiliations, board memberships, and advisory roles
   - Assess influence networks and power structures
   - Examine patterns in choice of associates and allies

6. **Reputation and Recognition**
   - Review peer recognition, awards, and professional honors
   - Assess media coverage patterns and public perception trends
   - Note any controversies, scandals, or ethical violations
   - Check professional standing, licenses, and credential verification
   - Evaluate reputation within relevant professional communities

7. **Integrity Assessment**
   - Evaluate consistency between words and actions over time
   - Assess honesty in admitting mistakes, limitations, or uncertainty
   - Check transparency in disclosure of conflicts and potential biases
   - Review ethical conduct patterns and decision-making under pressure
   - Examine responses to ethical dilemmas and accountability
   - Note any documented patterns of deception or misrepresentation
   - Assess adherence to professional codes of conduct

8. **Motivations and Worldview**
   - Identify core values and belief systems that drive decisions
   - Assess ideological commitments and philosophical foundations
   - Note any stated missions, goals, or long-term objectives
   - Examine what the subject appears to care most deeply about
   - Consider how personal history may shape current perspectives

## Elicitation Requirements

elicit: true
format: |
  Please specify:

  **Background Analysis Subject:**

  - Who or what organization needs background analysis?
  - What specific aspects of their background are most relevant to your content?
  - What time period should be covered (entire history or recent period)?

  **Analysis Focus:**

  - What specific questions need to be answered about this subject?
  - Are there particular areas of concern or special interest?
  - What level of detail is required (overview vs. deep investigation)?
  - How will this analysis be used in your content?

  **Research Parameters:**

  - What sources are available for research (public records, publications, interviews)?
  - Are there any restrictions on investigation methods or sources?
  - What verification standards should be applied?
  - What time constraints exist for this research?

## Output Requirements

Deliver comprehensive background analysis including:

- **Chronological Overview**: Timeline of key events, positions, and decisions
- **Credibility Assessment**: Evaluation of reliability patterns and track record
- **Integrity Evaluation**: Specific examples demonstrating ethical conduct patterns
- **Motivations Analysis**: Identification of likely motivations, incentives, and biases
- **Network Map**: Key relationships and spheres of influence
- **Strengths & Weaknesses**: Balanced assessment of demonstrated capabilities
- **Areas of Concern**: Red flags, contradictions, or unresolved questions
- **Contextual Significance**: How background informs current positions or claims
- **Source Documentation**: Citations for all key findings with assessment of source quality
