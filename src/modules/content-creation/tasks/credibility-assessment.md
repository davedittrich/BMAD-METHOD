# credibility-assessment

## Task Overview

Systematically evaluate the credibility and trustworthiness of individuals, organizations, or institutions using established assessment criteria to inform source evaluation and content creation decisions.

## Instructions

1. **Expertise Evaluation**
   - Assess relevant qualifications, certifications, and education
   - Review professional experience and track record in specific domain
   - Examine peer recognition, awards, and professional standing
   - Evaluate demonstrated specialized knowledge and expertise
   - Compare claimed expertise with actual credentials and performance

2. **Track Record Analysis**
   - Review history of accuracy and reliability in past statements
   - Examine how past mistakes, errors, or failures were handled
   - Assess consistency of performance and quality over time
   - Note patterns of success, failure, or improvement
   - Document specific examples of accurate and inaccurate predictions/claims

3. **Independence Assessment**
   - Identify potential conflicts of interest (financial, professional, personal)
   - Assess financial incentives, funding sources, and economic pressures
   - Evaluate institutional loyalties, obligations, and allegiances
   - Check for sources of bias and motivations that might affect judgment
   - Examine relationships that could compromise objectivity

4. **Transparency Evaluation**
   - Assess openness about methods, sources, and decision-making processes
   - Review disclosure of conflicts of interest and limitations
   - Examine willingness to admit uncertainty, gaps in knowledge, or errors
   - Note transparency in correction, retraction, and update policies
   - Evaluate accessibility of supporting evidence and documentation

5. **Verification and Accountability**
   - Check if claims can be independently verified through other sources
   - Assess accountability mechanisms in place (peer review, editorial oversight)
   - Review responsiveness to challenges, criticism, and contrary evidence
   - Examine correction and update practices when errors are discovered
   - Note whether the subject engages constructively with critics

6. **Contextual Factors**
   - Consider relevant situational pressures and constraints
   - Assess cultural, institutional, and professional context
   - Note any extraordinary circumstances affecting credibility
   - Evaluate available resources and support for quality work
   - Consider how context may affect reliability in specific situations

7. **Reputation and Reception**
   - Assess how the subject is regarded by peers and experts
   - Review critical reception of past work by credible sources
   - Note endorsements, criticisms, and controversies
   - Examine standing within relevant professional communities
   - Document any credibility-damaging incidents or scandals

format: |
  Please specify:

  **Assessment Subject:**

  - Who or what organization needs credibility assessment?
  - What specific claims, expertise, or context should be evaluated?
  - What time period or specific incidents should be examined?

  **Assessment Purpose:**

  - Why is this credibility assessment needed?
  - How will the results be used in your content?
  - What standards of credibility are required for your purpose?

  **Available Information:**

  - What information is available about the subject?
  - Are there specific sources, databases, or records to consult?
  - What level of investigation is possible or appropriate?
  - Any known red flags or concerns to investigate?

## Output Requirements

Deliver comprehensive credibility assessment including:

- **Detailed Evaluation**: Assessment against each criterion with specific evidence
- **Overall Credibility Rating**: Clear rating (high/medium/low) with detailed justification
- **Strengths**: Specific aspects supporting credibility and trustworthiness
- **Weaknesses**: Specific concerns, limitations, or credibility-damaging factors
- **Red Flags**: Any significant concerns requiring special attention
- **Contextual Considerations**: How context affects credibility assessment
- **Recommendations**:
  - Level of reliance appropriate for this source
  - Verification needs before using as source
  - Specific claims requiring additional corroboration
  - Usage guidelines for this source in content creation
- **Documentation**: Citations for all key findings with source quality assessment
