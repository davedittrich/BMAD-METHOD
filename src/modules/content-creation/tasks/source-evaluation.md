# source-evaluation

## Task Overview

Systematically evaluate the reliability, credibility, and usefulness of information sources to determine their suitability for research and reporting.

## Instructions

1. **Source Type Assessment**
   - Classify source type (primary, secondary, tertiary)
   - Identify publication type and format
   - Assess peer review status and editorial oversight
   - Note any special characteristics or limitations

2. **Author Credibility**
   - Evaluate author qualifications and expertise
   - Assess author's track record and reputation
   - Check for potential conflicts of interest
   - Note institutional affiliations and funding sources

3. **Publication Credibility**
   - Assess reputation and standards of publication venue
   - Check editorial policies and fact-checking procedures
   - Evaluate peer review processes if applicable
   - Note circulation, impact, and professional standing

4. **Content Quality**
   - Assess accuracy and factual reliability
   - Check for supporting evidence and documentation
   - Evaluate methodology and research quality
   - Note any bias, agenda, or perspective issues

5. **Timeliness and Relevance**
   - Assess currency and recency of information
   - Check for updates, corrections, or retractions
   - Evaluate relevance to current research needs
   - Note any time-sensitive limitations

6. **Verification and Corroboration**
   - Check if information can be independently verified
   - Assess availability of corroborating sources
   - Note any contradictory information from other sources
   - Evaluate consistency with established knowledge

## Elicitation Requirements

elicit: true
format: |
Please specify:

**Source Evaluation Parameters:**

- What specific sources need evaluation?
- What research topic or question are these sources supporting?
- What level of reliability is required for the intended use?

**Evaluation Criteria:**

- Are there specific credibility standards to apply?
- What type of information is being sought from these sources?
- Are there any particular concerns or red flags to check?

**Usage Context:**

- How will these sources be used in research or reporting?
- Who is the intended audience for the final work?
- What are the consequences of using unreliable sources?

## Output Requirements

Deliver comprehensive source evaluation including:

- Detailed credibility assessment for each source
- Reliability rating with supporting justification
- Identification of strengths and limitations
- Recommendations for appropriate use or additional verification needed
- Summary ranking of sources by reliability and usefulness
