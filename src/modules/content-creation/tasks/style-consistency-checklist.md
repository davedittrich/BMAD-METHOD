# Style Consistency Checklist

Use this checklist to evaluate consistency in writing style, voice, and presentation across multiple pieces of content or over time.

## Voice and Tone Consistency

### Voice Characteristics

- [ ] **Personality consistency**: Same underlying personality evident across samples
- [ ] **Authority level**: Consistent level of authority and expertise projection
- [ ] **Formality level**: Consistent formality/informality across appropriate contexts
- [ ] **Perspective consistency**: Consistent first/second/third person usage patterns
- [ ] **Emotional register**: Consistent emotional tone and expression patterns

### Tone Variations

- [ ] **Appropriate adaptation**: Tone appropriately adapts to context while maintaining core voice
- [ ] **Audience awareness**: Consistent awareness of audience needs and expectations
- [ ] **Subject matter alignment**: Tone consistently aligns with subject matter seriousness
- [ ] **Platform adaptation**: Appropriate tone adaptation for different platforms/media
- [ ] **Situational awareness**: Tone responds appropriately to situational demands

### Documentation

- [ ] **Voice examples**: Specific examples of consistent voice characteristics
- [ ] **Tone range**: Documentation of appropriate tone range and variations
- [ ] **Context mapping**: How voice/tone adapts to different contexts
- [ ] **Inconsistencies noted**: Any significant voice or tone inconsistencies

## Language and Vocabulary Consistency

### Vocabulary Level

- [ ] **Complexity consistency**: Consistent vocabulary sophistication level
- [ ] **Technical language**: Consistent use of technical or specialized terms
- [ ] **Accessibility level**: Consistent accessibility for target audience
- [ ] **Jargon usage**: Consistent approach to professional or industry jargon
- [ ] **Regional variations**: Consistent regional or cultural language patterns

### Word Choice Patterns

- [ ] **Synonym preferences**: Consistent preferences for particular synonyms
- [ ] **Verb tense patterns**: Consistent verb tense usage preferences
- [ ] **Active vs. passive**: Consistent voice preferences (active/passive)
- [ ] **Intensifier usage**: Consistent use of intensifying words and phrases
- [ ] **Qualifying language**: Consistent use of hedging or qualifying language

### Documentation

- [ ] **Vocabulary samples**: Examples of consistent vocabulary patterns
- [ ] **Preference patterns**: Documented preferences for specific word choices
- [ ] **Evolution tracking**: Changes in vocabulary over time if applicable
- [ ] **Audience adaptation**: How vocabulary adapts appropriately to different audiences

- [ ] **Complexity patterns**: Consistent approach to sentence complexity
- [ ] **Punctuation habits**: Consistent punctuation usage and style
- [ ] **Rhythm patterns**: Consistent rhythmic and flow patterns
- [ ] **Parallel structure**: Consistent use of parallel construction

### Paragraph Organization

- [ ] **Length preferences**: Consistent paragraph length patterns
- [ ] **Topic organization**: Consistent approach to organizing ideas within paragraphs
- [ ] **Transition methods**: Consistent methods for connecting paragraphs
- [ ] **Introduction patterns**: Consistent paragraph opening strategies
- [ ] **Conclusion patterns**: Consistent paragraph closing approaches

### Document Structure

- [ ] **Opening strategies**: Consistent approaches to beginning documents/content
- [ ] **Section organization**: Consistent methods for organizing longer content
- [ ] **Closing strategies**: Consistent approaches to ending content
- [ ] **Header usage**: Consistent use of headers and subheaders
- [ ] **List formatting**: Consistent formatting of lists and bullet points

### Documentation

- [ ] **Structure examples**: Examples of consistent structural patterns
- [ ] **Organization methods**: Documented organizational preferences and methods
- [ ] **Format consistency**: Evidence of consistent formatting choices
- [ ] **Evolution patterns**: How structure has evolved appropriately over time

### External Style Guides

- [ ] **Style guide identification**: Clear identification of style guide(s) being followed
- [ ] **Consistent application**: Consistent adherence to chosen style guide rules
- [ ] **Exception handling**: Consistent approach to style guide exceptions
- [ ] **Update compliance**: Adherence to style guide updates and changes
- [ ] **Citation consistency**: Consistent citation and reference formatting

### Internal Style Consistency

- [ ] **Personal conventions**: Consistent application of personal style conventions
- [ ] **Brand alignment**: Consistency with organizational or brand style requirements
- [ ] **Platform requirements**: Consistent adaptation to platform-specific style needs
- [ ] **Evolution documentation**: Changes in style approach documented and consistent
- [ ] **Preference rationale**: Clear reasoning for style choices and preferences

### Documentation

- [ ] **Compliance examples**: Examples of consistent style guide adherence
- [ ] **Exception documentation**: Documented exceptions to style guide rules
- [ ] **Evolution tracking**: Documentation of style evolution over time

- [ ] **Spacing and layout**: Consistent spacing and layout preferences

- [ ] **Perspective maintenance**: Consistent viewpoint and perspective
- [ ] **Message consistency**: Core messages remain consistent across content
- [ ] **Priority consistency**: Consistent prioritization of themes and topics
- [ ] **Evolution coherence**: Thematic evolution is coherent and explained

- [ ] **Source consistency**: Consistent approach to sourcing and verification
- [ ] **Update practices**: Consistent updating of information as facts change
- [ ] **Correction handling**: Consistent approach to correcting factual errors

- [ ] **Fact verification**: Evidence of consistent fact-checking practices
- [ ] **Message alignment**: Documentation of core message consistency

## Context Adaptation Consistency

### Audience Adaptation

- [ ] **Appropriate adjustment**: Style appropriately adjusts for different audiences
- [ ] **Core maintenance**: Core voice maintained while adapting to audience needs
- [ ] **Respect consistency**: Consistent respect for audience intelligence and needs
- [ ] **Accessibility consideration**: Consistent consideration of accessibility needs
- [ ] **Cultural sensitivity**: Consistent cultural sensitivity across contexts

### Platform Adaptation

- [ ] **Platform optimization**: Appropriate adaptation to platform requirements
- [ ] **Brand consistency**: Brand voice maintained across platforms
- [ ] **Technical adaptation**: Appropriate technical adaptations (length, format, etc.)
- [ ] **Engagement consistency**: Consistent engagement strategies across platforms
- [ ] **Quality maintenance**: Quality standards maintained across all platforms

### Documentation

- [ ] **Adaptation examples**: Examples of appropriate context adaptation
- [ ] **Consistency maintenance**: Evidence of core consistency despite adaptation
- [ ] **Platform optimization**: Documentation of platform-specific optimizations
- [ ] **Quality standards**: Consistent quality standards across contexts

## Overall Consistency Assessment

### Consistency Strengths

- [ ] **Core voice maintenance**: Strong maintenance of core voice and identity
- [ ] **Appropriate adaptation**: Skillful adaptation to context while maintaining consistency
- [ ] **Quality standards**: Consistent maintenance of quality standards
- [ ] **Professional presentation**: Consistent professional presentation and formatting
- [ ] **Brand alignment**: Strong alignment with brand or personal identity

### Consistency Concerns

- [ ] **Inconsistent elements**: Identification of inconsistent elements and patterns
- [ ] **Quality variations**: Variations in quality or standards across content
- [ ] **Voice drift**: Gradual changes in voice or style without clear rationale
- [ ] **Context confusion**: Inappropriate style for context or audience
- [ ] **Technical inconsistencies**: Inconsistent technical standards or formatting

### Recommendations

- [ ] **Consistency improvements**: Specific areas where consistency can be improved
- [ ] **Style guide needs**: Recommendations for style guide development or adherence
- [ ] **Quality control**: Suggestions for improving quality control processes
- [ ] **Training needs**: Areas where additional training or development is needed
- [ ] **Monitoring practices**: Recommendations for ongoing consistency monitoring

## Documentation Requirements

- [ ] **Sample analysis**: Analysis of representative content samples for consistency
- [ ] **Pattern documentation**: Clear documentation of identified consistency patterns
- [ ] **Inconsistency tracking**: Documentation of inconsistencies and their patterns
- [ ] **Evolution mapping**: Timeline of style evolution and changes
- [ ] **Recommendation basis**: Clear evidence basis for consistency recommendations
