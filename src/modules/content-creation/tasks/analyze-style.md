# analyze-style

## Task Overview

Systematically analyze writing samples to extract and document style patterns, voice characteristics, and linguistic preferences for consistency checking and style replication in content creation.

## Instructions

1. **Style Pattern Analysis**
   - Identify sentence structure preferences (simple, complex, compound-complex)
   - Analyze paragraph organization patterns and typical lengths
   - Note transition patterns, connecting phrases, and flow techniques
   - Examine overall pacing preferences (fast-paced vs. contemplative)
   - Document structural patterns (use of lists, headers, quotes, examples)

2. **Voice Characteristics**
   - Determine predominant tone (formal, casual, conversational, authoritative, etc.)
   - Identify personality traits reflected in writing (humorous, serious, empathetic)
   - Note emotional register and expression patterns
   - Assess level of directness vs. diplomatic/hedging language
   - Identify narrative perspective (first person, second person, third person)

3. **Vocabulary and Diction Analysis**
   - Identify commonly used words, phrases, and verbal habits
   - Note specialized terminology and jargon usage patterns
   - Analyze vocabulary complexity level and approximate reading grade
   - Document unique expressions, catchphrases, or signature language
   - Assess formality level of word choices

4. **Grammar and Mechanics Patterns**
   - Note punctuation preferences (em-dashes, semicolons, ellipses, etc.)
   - Identify grammar quirks or consistent stylistic choices
   - Analyze active vs. passive voice ratio and preferences
   - Document capitalization patterns and formatting preferences
   - Note sentence length variation and rhythm patterns

5. **Rhetorical Devices and Techniques**
   - Identify use of metaphors, analogies, and similes
   - Note question usage (rhetorical questions, direct questions)
   - Analyze humor patterns, irony, wit, and sarcasm usage
   - Document storytelling techniques and narrative elements
   - Assess use of repetition, parallelism, and emphasis techniques

6. **Audience Adaptation Patterns**
   - Assess how style changes across different contexts or platforms
   - Note formality level adjustments for different audiences
   - Identify elements that remain consistent across all contexts
   - Document platform-specific adaptations (Twitter vs. long-form, etc.)

7. **Content Organization**
   - Analyze typical article/piece structure and flow
   - Note opening and closing techniques
   - Identify how arguments or points are typically developed
   - Document use of examples, evidence, and supporting material
   - Assess balance between exposition, analysis, and storytelling

## Elicitation Requirements

elicit: true
format: |
Please provide:

**Writing Samples:**

- What writing samples should be analyzed? (provide URLs, file paths, or text)
- How many samples are available? (minimum 3 recommended)
- What is the approximate total word count to analyze?
- Are these all from the same author?

**Sample Context:**

- What formats/platforms do these samples come from? (blog, academic, social media, etc.)
- What is the date range of these samples?
- Are there different contexts or genres to compare?

**Analysis Purpose:**

- Is this for replicating the style in new content?
- Is this for consistency checking across existing content?
- What level of detail is needed in the style profile?

## Output Requirements

Create comprehensive style analysis profile including:

- Detailed breakdown of all style pattern categories above
- Voice and tone characterization with specific examples
- Style guide document for replication or consistency checking
- Representative quotations illustrating key style elements
- Quantitative metrics where applicable (avg sentence length, vocabulary grade level, etc.)
- Recommendations for maintaining consistency
- List of style "rules" or preferences extracted from samples
