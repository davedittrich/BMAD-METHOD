# source-verification

## Task Overview

Evaluate the credibility, reliability, and trustworthiness of sources using systematic assessment criteria.

## Instructions

1. **Initial Source Assessment**
   - Identify the type of source (academic, news, government, personal, etc.)
   - Determine the author or organization behind the source
   - Check publication date and currency of information
   - Assess the source's reputation and track record

2. **Author/Organization Credibility**
   - Research author's expertise and qualifications
   - Check institutional affiliations and credentials
   - Review track record of accuracy and reliability
   - Identify any potential conflicts of interest

3. **Publication Standards**
   - Evaluate editorial oversight and fact-checking processes
   - Assess peer review or professional editing standards
   - Check correction and retraction policies
   - Review transparency about funding and ownership

4. **Content Quality Assessment**
   - Verify proper citation and source attribution
   - Check for evidence-based claims vs. opinion
   - Assess logical consistency and internal coherence
   - Look for bias indicators and balanced perspective

5. **Cross-Reference Verification**
   - Compare information across multiple sources
   - Check if other credible sources corroborate claims
   - Identify any contradicting reliable sources
   - Assess consensus among experts in the field

## Elicitation Requirements

- Get specific sources to be evaluated
- Understand the context and purpose of verification
- Clarify standards for acceptable source credibility
- Confirm available resources for background research

## Output Requirements

Produce detailed source credibility assessment including:

- Credibility rating and justification
- Specific strengths and weaknesses identified
- Recommendations for use or caution
- Supporting documentation for assessment
