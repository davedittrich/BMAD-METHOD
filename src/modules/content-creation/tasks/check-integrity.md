# check-integrity

## Task Overview

Perform final quality assurance check on completed content to verify integrity consistency using Stephen L. Carter's framework, ensuring the piece meets all three integrity criteria before publication.

## Instructions

1. **Pre-Check Preparation**
   - Review the complete content from beginning to end
   - Identify all moral claims, value judgments, and ethical positions taken
   - Note all recommendations, advocated actions, or policy positions
   - Create inventory of key integrity-sensitive elements

2. **Discernment Check (Criterion 1)**
   - Verify moral dimensions are properly identified and addressed
   - Confirm ethical considerations are not ignored or minimized
   - Check that competing values are acknowledged
   - Ensure consequences and implications are discussed
   - Pass/Fail: Does content demonstrate moral awareness?

3. **Acting/Acknowledgment Check (Criterion 2)**
   - Verify consistency between stated values and advocated actions
   - Confirm costs and trade-offs are honestly acknowledged
   - Check for hypocrisy between different sections or claims
   - Ensure principles are maintained throughout, not abandoned for convenience
   - Verify willingness to accept consequences of moral positions
   - Pass/Fail: Are values and actions aligned?

4. **Transparency Check (Criterion 3)**
   - Verify moral reasoning is explicit and traceable
   - Confirm positions can be defended on moral grounds
   - Check for clear responsibility-taking and accountability
   - Ensure no obscuring or hiding of ethical dimensions
   - Verify openness about assumptions and value judgments
   - Pass/Fail: Is moral reasoning transparent?

5. **Corruption Indicator Scan**
   - Check for moral relativism or convenient ethics shifts
   - Scan for double standards (in-group vs. out-group)
   - Identify any blame-shifting or responsibility evasion
   - Flag treating ethics as PR rather than genuine principle
   - Note any secrecy around decision-making or reasoning
   - Alert: List all corruption indicators found (0 = pass)

6. **Pattern Consistency Verification**
   - Verify integrity is maintained throughout entire piece
   - Check beginning, middle, and end for consistency
   - Ensure no integrity lapses in any section
   - Confirm thesis and conclusion align on integrity grounds
   - Pass/Fail: Is integrity consistent throughout?

7. **Final Assessment and Certification**
   - Assign overall integrity rating (Pass/Fail or 1-10 scale)
   - List all integrity issues requiring correction (blocking issues)
   - List recommended improvements (non-blocking suggestions)
   - Provide integrity certification or revision requirements
   - Document decision: Ready for publication OR Needs revision

## Output Requirements

Deliver comprehensive QA integrity check including:

- Pass/Fail assessment for each of Carter's three criteria
- Detailed inventory of all integrity issues found (blocking and non-blocking)
- List of corruption indicators identified with specific examples
- Specific corrections required for publication approval
- Recommended improvements for strengthening integrity
- Overall integrity certification decision (Approved/Revision Required)
- Quantitative integrity score if requested (1-10 scale)
- Documentation trail for integrity verification
