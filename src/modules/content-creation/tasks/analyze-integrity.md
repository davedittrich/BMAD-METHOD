# analyze-integrity

## Task Overview

Apply Stephen L. Carter's integrity framework to analyze written content, arguments, or positions for integrity consistency using the three-step analysis: discernment, acting, and saying openly.

## Instructions

1. **Step 1: Discernment Assessment**
   - Evaluate whether content demonstrates moral awareness
   - Identify ethical dimensions and competing values discussed
   - Assess understanding of consequences and implications
   - Look for evidence of distinguishing right from wrong
   - Document examples showing moral reflection or blindness

2. **Step 2: Acting/Acknowledgment Assessment**
   - Examine consistency between stated values and advocated actions
   - Assess willingness to acknowledge costs of doing right
   - Identify patterns of moral compromise or principle abandonment
   - Check for hypocrisy between claims and recommendations
   - Note whether principles hold under pressure or convenience

3. **Step 3: Saying Openly/Transparency Assessment**
   - Evaluate transparency in moral reasoning presented
   - Assess ability to defend positions on moral grounds
   - Look for accountability and responsibility-taking in content
   - Check public vs. private behavior alignment (if applicable)
   - Identify attempts to obscure or hide moral dimensions

4. **Pattern Analysis**
   - Identify consistent integrity patterns across content
   - Note any shifts in moral reasoning over time
   - Assess responses to ethical challenges presented
   - Evaluate learning from past moral positions

5. **Corruption Indicators Check**
   - Moral relativism when convenient
   - Different standards for in-group vs. out-group
   - Secrecy or evasion around ethical reasoning
   - Blame-shifting when confronted with inconsistencies
   - Treating ethics as PR rather than principle

6. **Documentation and Evidence**
   - Compile specific examples and quotations
   - Cross-reference claims with observable patterns
   - Verify accuracy of characterizations
   - Maintain objectivity in assessment
