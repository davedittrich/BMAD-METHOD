# extract-voice-patterns

## Task Overview

Analyze writing samples to extract distinctive voice patterns, style characteristics, and linguistic fingerprints for consistency analysis, authorship verification, and voice replication in content creation.

## Instructions

### 1. Linguistic Pattern Analysis

**Sentence Structure:**

- Identify sentence structure preferences and patterns (simple, compound, complex)
- Analyze average sentence length and variation patterns
- Examine clause structure and subordination preferences
- Note rhythm and cadence patterns in writing

**Vocabulary Patterns:**

- Analyze vocabulary choices and complexity levels
- Identify frequently used words and phrases
- Examine specialized terminology and jargon usage
- Note unique or signature expressions

**Punctuation and Formatting:**

- Examine punctuation habits (commas, semicolons, em-dashes, ellipses)
- Analyze formatting preferences and patterns
- Note capitalization habits and conventions
- Identify spacing, indentation, and layout patterns

**Language Variation:**

- Note dialect, colloquialisms, or regional variations
- Identify multilingual elements or code-switching
- Examine formal vs. colloquial language balance
- Document any distinctive linguistic quirks

### 2. Style Characteristics

**Tone and Emotional Register:**

- Assess tone patterns (serious, humorous, sarcastic, earnest)
- Identify emotional register and intensity
- Examine level of formality across contexts
- Note warmth, distance, or personality projection

**Humor and Wit:**

- Examine use of humor, sarcasm, or irony
- Identify wit patterns and comedic techniques
- Note playfulness vs. seriousness balance
- Document signature humorous expressions

**Rhetorical Techniques:**

- Note persuasive techniques and rhetorical devices
- Identify use of questions (rhetorical, direct)
- Examine appeals (logos, pathos, ethos)
- Assess argumentation style and structure

**Voice Personality:**

- Identify personality traits reflected in writing
- Assess authority, confidence, or humility levels
- Note empathy, warmth, or analytical distance
- Document overall voice "character"

### 3. Structural Elements

**Organization Patterns:**

- Analyze paragraph organization and typical lengths
- Examine transition and flow preferences
- Identify how ideas are sequenced and connected
- Note structural frameworks (chronological, thematic, etc.)

**Opening and Closing Styles:**

- Identify introduction patterns and hooks
- Examine conclusion styles and techniques
- Note signature openings or closings
- Assess symmetry and structure preferences

**Formatting Preferences:**

- Note use of headers, subheaders, and sections
- Identify list usage patterns (numbered, bulleted)
- Examine use of emphasis (bold, italics, quotes)
- Document visual organization preferences

### 4. Content Patterns

**Themes and Topics:**

- Identify recurring themes and subject interests
- Examine topic selection and coverage patterns
- Note areas of expertise or focus
- Assess topical consistency over time

**Perspective and Viewpoint:**

- Examine perspective consistency (first person, third person)
- Assess objectivity vs. subjectivity balance
- Note ideological or philosophical leanings
- Identify core values reflected in writing

**Evidence and Support:**

- Note citation and reference habits
- Assess use of examples and illustrations
- Examine how claims are supported
- Identify preferred evidence types (data, anecdotes, authority)

### 5. Technical Elements

**Grammar and Syntax:**

- Analyze grammar patterns and preferences
- Examine syntax complexity and variation
- Note consistent grammatical choices
- Identify any deliberate grammar "violations" for effect

**Error Patterns:**

- Examine error patterns (if any) and corrections
- Note any consistent "mistakes" that may be stylistic
- Assess evolution of technical precision over time

**Composition Habits:**

- Note capitalization habits and conventions
- Identify typing or composition quirks
- Examine editing traces and revision patterns
- Document any technical fingerprints

### 6. Voice Evolution and Adaptation

**Temporal Changes:**

- Track changes in voice over time periods
- Note style evolution and development
- Identify consistent core characteristics across time
- Assess maturation or refinement of voice

**Contextual Adaptation:**

- Note adaptations for different audiences
- Examine platform-specific voice modifications
- Identify formal vs. informal context variations
- Assess flexibility vs. consistency balance

**Authenticity Assessment:**

- Identify authentic core voice elements
- Assess authenticity versus adaptation
- Note when voice feels forced or natural
- Document "true voice" characteristics

## Output Requirements

Deliver comprehensive voice pattern analysis including:

### 1. Voice Pattern Profile

- **Linguistic Fingerprint**: Distinctive language patterns and markers
- **Style Signature**: Unique style characteristics that define the voice
- **Voice DNA**: Core elements that remain consistent across all contexts
- **Distinctive Markers**: Unique identifiers that separate this voice from others

### 2. Detailed Analysis

- **Linguistic Patterns**: Sentence structure, vocabulary, punctuation, language variation
- **Style Characteristics**: Tone, humor, rhetoric, personality
- **Structural Elements**: Organization, openings/closings, formatting
- **Content Patterns**: Themes, perspective, evidence preferences
- **Technical Elements**: Grammar, syntax, composition habits

### 3. Pattern Documentation

- **Representative Examples**: Quotations illustrating key patterns
- **Quantitative Metrics**: Average sentence length, vocabulary level, etc.
- **Frequency Analysis**: Most common words, phrases, constructions
- **Pattern Consistency**: Assessment of consistency across samples

### 4. Voice Evolution (if applicable)

- **Timeline Analysis**: How voice has changed over time
- **Context Adaptation**: How voice adjusts for different audiences/platforms
- **Core Consistency**: Elements that remain unchanged
- **Evolution Assessment**: Growth, refinement, or drift in voice

### 5. Practical Applications

- **Voice Replication Guide**: How to write in this voice
- **Consistency Checklist**: Key elements to check for authenticity
- **Red Flags**: What would indicate a different author
- **Verification Criteria**: How to confirm authorship

### 6. Supporting Data

- **Sample Inventory**: List of all samples analyzed with metadata
- **Analysis Methodology**: Approach and frameworks used
- **Confidence Assessment**: Reliability of findings based on sample size
- **Recommendations**: Suggestions for additional analysis if needed
