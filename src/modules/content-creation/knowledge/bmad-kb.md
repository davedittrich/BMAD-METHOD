# BMAD Content Creation Knowledge Base

## Overview

BMAD Content Creation Module adapts the BMAD-Method framework for producing social media content, independent journalism, research-driven articles, and analytical responses to published content. This module provides specialized agents, workflows, and tools designed specifically for content creators, journalists, and researchers.

### Key Features

- **Research Agents**: Background research specific to topics, people, events, and organizations
- **Fact-Checking Framework**: Systematic verification using the Carter integrity framework
- **Complete Research Workflows**: From initial research to publication-ready content
- **Platform-Specific Optimization**: Tailored for social media, blogs, YouTube, and publishing platforms
- **Response Journalism**: Structured approach to analyzing and responding to others' published work
- **Integrity Analysis**: Ethical framework for evaluating sources and subjects

### When to Use BMAD Content Creation

- **Independent Journalism**: Research-driven investigative reporting
- **Social Media Content**: Video scripts, Twitter threads, platform-optimized posts
- **Response Articles**: Analytical responses to published content with fact-checking
- **Content Creation**: Blog posts, newsletter content, educational material
- **Research Projects**: Systematic investigation of complex topics

## How BMAD Content Creation Works

### The Core Method

BMAD Content Creation transforms you into a "Research Director" - orchestrating specialized AI agents through the research and writing process:

1. **You Guide, AI Researches**: You provide direction; agents handle systematic investigation
2. **Specialized Agents**: Each agent masters one aspect (research, fact-checking, analysis, writing)
3. **Structured Workflows**: Proven journalistic patterns guide your process
4. **Integrity-Focused**: Carter framework ensures ethical standards throughout

### The Three-Phase Approach

#### Phase 1: Research & Analysis

- Strategic research planning and source identification
- Background context gathering on topics, people, and organizations
- Subject analysis including credibility assessment and motivation analysis
- Timeline development for events and relationships

#### Phase 2: Verification & Synthesis

- Fact-checking using multiple independent sources
- Logical analysis of arguments and claims
- Integrity assessment using Carter framework
- Cross-referencing and contradiction identification

#### Phase 3: Creation & Optimization

- Content creation maintaining journalistic standards
- Platform-specific optimization for target audience
- Style consistency with user's voice and approach
- Final quality assurance and publication preparation

## Research Methods

### Source Evaluation Framework

- **Author Expertise**: Relevant qualifications and track record
- **Publication Standards**: Editorial process and reputation
- **Bias Assessment**: Financial interests and independence evaluation
- **Evidence Quality**: Source citation and verification capability
- **Currency Assessment**: Information timeliness and relevance

### Fact-Checking Process

1. **Claim Identification**: Extract specific factual claims
2. **Source Verification**: Verify claims against primary sources
3. **Cross-Reference**: Check against multiple independent sources
4. **Expert Consultation**: Seek relevant expert opinion where needed
5. **Documentation**: Maintain clear record of verification process

### Integrity Analysis (Carter Framework)

1. **Discernment**: Can subject distinguish right from wrong?
2. **Acknowledgment**: Does subject act according to their discernment?
3. **Action Consistency**: Can subject defend actions on moral grounds?
