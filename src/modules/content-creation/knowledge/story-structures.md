# Article and Content Structure Patterns

## Inverted Pyramid Structure (News)

- **Lead (10-15%)**: Most important information first - who, what, when, where, why
- **Body (70-80%)**: Supporting details in descending order of importance
- **Conclusion (5-15%)**: Additional context or future implications

## Feature Article Structure

- **Hook (5-10%)**: Compelling opening that draws reader in
- **Nut Graf (5-10%)**: Core thesis and why it matters now
- **Development (70-80%)**: Supporting evidence, quotes, analysis
- **Kicker (5-10%)**: Memorable ending that reinforces main point

## Investigative Reporting Structure

1. **Opening Scene**: Specific incident that illustrates the problem
2. **Problem Statement**: Clear articulation of what's wrong
3. **Evidence Presentation**: Systematic documentation of findings
4. **Expert Analysis**: Professional interpretation of evidence
5. **Subject Response**: Fair opportunity for response/rebuttal
6. **Broader Context**: How this fits larger patterns or trends
7. **Impact Assessment**: Consequences and implications
8. **Resolution Path**: What can be done about it

## Opinion/Editorial Structure

1. **Hook**: Attention-grabbing opening
2. **Position Statement**: Clear thesis
3. **Evidence Block 1**: Strongest supporting argument
4. **Evidence Block 2**: Additional supporting evidence
5. **Counter-argument**: Acknowledging opposing views
6. **Rebuttal**: Why your position remains stronger
7. **Call to Action**: What readers should think or do

## Explainer Article Structure

1. **Why This Matters**: Immediate relevance to reader
2. **Background Context**: Essential historical/situational context
3. **Key Players**: Who's involved and their roles
4. **Current Situation**: What's happening right now
5. **Multiple Perspectives**: Different stakeholder viewpoints
6. **Looking Forward**: What to expect next

## Social Media Content Patterns

### Thread Structure

- **Hook Tweet**: Attention-grabbing opener with thread indicator
- **Context Setting**: Background needed to understand
- **Main Content**: 3-7 tweets developing the core message
- **Evidence/Examples**: Specific supporting details
- **Conclusion**: Summary and call-to-action

### Video Script Structure

- **Hook (0-15 seconds)**: Immediate engagement
- **Preview (15-30 seconds)**: What viewer will learn
- **Main Content (30-90%)**: Core information delivery
- **Recap (5-10%)**: Key takeaways summary
- **Call-to-Action**: What to do next (subscribe, share, comment)

## Response Article Structure

1. **Context Setting**: Briefly summarize what you're responding to
2. **Credibility Assessment**: Evaluate source credibility
3. **Fact Verification**: Check key claims against evidence
4. **Logical Analysis**: Examine reasoning and arguments
5. **Additional Context**: Provide missing background information
6. **Alternative Perspective**: Present different viewpoint with evidence
7. **Conclusion**: Summary of findings and recommendations

## Research Article Structure

1. **Research Question**: What you're trying to understand
2. **Methodology**: How you conducted your investigation
3. **Findings**: What you discovered, organized by theme
2. **Analysis**: What the findings mean and their implications
3. **Limitations**: What you couldn't determine or verify
4. **Next Steps**: Areas for further investigation
