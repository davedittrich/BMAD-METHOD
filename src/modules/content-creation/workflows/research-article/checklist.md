# Research Article Checklist

## Research

- [ ] Topic is clearly defined.
- [ ] Sufficient research has been conducted.
- [ ] Sources are credible and properly cited.

## Writing

- [ ] Article has a clear and compelling introduction.
- [ ] Body of the article is well-structured and easy to follow.
- [ ] Conclusion summarizes the main points and provides a clear takeaway.

## Editing

- [ ] Article has been proofread for grammar, spelling, and punctuation errors.
- [ ] Article is consistent with the established style guide.

## Fact-Checking

- [ ] All claims are supported by evidence.
- [ ] All sources have been verified.
